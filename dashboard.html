Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reschedule an appointment?

You can reschedule by going to "My Appointments", finding your upcoming visit, and clicking the "Reschedule" button. Note that reschedules must happen at least 24 hours prior.

How do I request a prescription refill?

Navigate to the "Prescriptions" tab. If your prescription has remaining refills, the "Request Refill" button will be active to submit your request directly to your pharmacy.

Can I pay my bill over the phone?

Yes, you can call our billing department at 1-800-555-BILL between 8 AM and 5 PM EST.
